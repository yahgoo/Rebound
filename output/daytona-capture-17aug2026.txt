## For video production workstream (Cursor mandate)

## Scope — what this capture does and does not show

**What WAS captured:** The AI-generated scoring step running in real Daytona sandboxes — 8 sandboxes provisioned concurrently with the Daytona API, executing a scoring function against 12 synthetic fixture candidates, producing a ranked list identical to LocalExecutor (PARITY OK). Real provisioning timings are shown below (majority ~1.5-2.0s, with two outliers at ~22s from resource contention). Zero sandboxes survived after cleanup.

**What was NOT captured:** A full search → verify → confirm → order → pay recovery case executing through Daytona end to end. This capture exercises only the scoring isolation (Zone B) — the step where model-generated ranking code runs in a sandbox with no network access. The surrounding search, verify, order, and pay stages are unrelated to Daytona and run through the Atlas transport layer regardless of EXECUTOR setting.

**Badging requirement for video:** Must be badged as "real Daytona sandbox execution of the scoring step" — not as "the full recovery ran on Daytona." Mislabeling this as an end-to-end Daytona capture would repeat the exact judge-critique defect this mandate is fixing.

## Setup
- Target slots: 8
- Candidates: 12 synthetic fixtures (no live Atlas spend required — scoring is data-independent)
- Scoring code: hand-written stdlib-only, identical to smoke_parity parity test
- Daytona API key: configured in .env
